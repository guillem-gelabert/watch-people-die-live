# Milestone v2.0: Persona Realism

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-08-28
**Phases:** 4-6 (Phase 6 INSERTED)
**Total Plans:** 13
**Archived:** 2026-08-28

## Overview

Persona age, sex and cause stopped being one global table. Where v1.0 drew every death from a
single age-flat cause distribution and a single national age pyramid, v2.0 made all three vary by
region and by season — 183 country-specific age-banded cause tables, a per-cell age/sex pyramid
resolver over 59,954 grid cells in three tiers, and a measured cause×month and age×month tensor
that reweights composition by the month the globe is already simulating.

Phase 5 ran concurrently and disjointly, fixing three story defects at `/roadmap`. Phase 6 was
inserted from the milestone audit, which returned `gaps_found` on its first pass and forced the
milestone to close on measurement rather than assertion.

## Phases

### Phase 4: Persona Realism Ladder

**Goal**: Persona age, sex and cause vary by region and by season, instead of every death drawing from one global, age-flat cause table and one national age pyramid.
**Feature:** Personas
**Mode:** v2
**Depends on**: Phase 1 (cause data foundation)
**Requirements**: [PERS-01, PERS-02, PERS-03, REAL-01, REAL-03]
**Plans**: 9 plans (grew from 8 when 04-09 was added)

**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

1. An infant persona never draws an adult-only cause; the `coverage` flag written by the builder is read by the consumer.
2. Cause distributions are country-specific for all 183 countries WHO GHE covers, including those without vital registration.
3. Age/sex distributions resolve per grid cell rather than per country, validated against observed regional counts for at least four countries.
4. Persona composition shifts with the season the globe is already simulating.
5. Estimated inputs are labelled as estimates and excluded from validation statistics.

Ordering was by wave, not plan number. Plan numbers preserve the captured priority ranking p01-p08
(p09 became backlog 999.1); waves came from pairwise `files_modified` overlap analysis, so no two
plans in a wave touch a shared path. `app/globe/persona.ts` was the chokepoint — 04-01, 04-04 and
04-07 all modify it, so they could not share a wave. Max parallelism was 3, in wave 2.

Plans:

- [x] 04-01: Honour the cause coverage flag in pickCause _(wave 1)_
- [x] 04-02: Source country age/sex causes from WHO Global Health Estimates _(wave 2)_
- [x] 04-05: Bake an admin-1 / NUTS-2 region key into the rate grid _(wave 2)_
- [x] 04-08: Unfilter age/sex in pipelines that already download it _(wave 2)_
- [x] 04-03: Pull GBD subnational age/sex death weights _(wave 3)_
- [x] 04-04: Resolve age/sex per cell from regional estimates and gridded population _(wave 4)_
- [x] 04-06: Add Eurostat regional age/sex and cause tables _(wave 4)_
- [x] 04-07: Make persona composition seasonal _(wave 5)_
- [x] 04-09: Rebuild tier 2 on WorldPop 1km population, replacing the CDR-gap proxy _(wave 6)_

**Delivered:**

- `pickCause` now reads the `coverage` flag the builder has always written, so a global all-ages cause export is rejected instead of handing infants a pensioner's cause.
- Every one of 183 countries has its own cause distribution per age band and sex, from WHO's keyless CC BY API, replacing a single global age-flat table.
- 444 of GBD's 508 leaf subnational units — 98.44% of subnational deaths — carry age × sex weights joined to the committed admin-1 / NUTS-2 key space, from a single 31,812-row export.
- Every populated grid cell carries the same `geo`+`key` region identity `subnational-cdr.json` uses, assigned by area majority on a sub-cell lattice, with 0.081% of expected deaths left explicitly unassigned.
- 291 NUTS-2 regions have observed age × sex, weekly and cause distributions from three Eurostat tables.
- Four pipeline sources were fetching age, sex and (Brazil, Mexico) cause and discarding them at parse time; they now emit 16,434 observed region × band × sex cells over 15,907,933 deaths, with the seasonality output byte-identical.
- A three-tier per-cell age/sex pyramid resolver, shipped as `data/age-sex-cells.json` (20 archetypes, classId + tier per rate-grid cell), with the cell index threaded from the sampler through to `makePersona()`.
- Tier 2 rebuilt as population × national rate from WorldPop 1km for 72 countries (51.75% of world expected deaths), replacing the single-degree-of-freedom CDR-gap proxy — with a measured, honest partial verdict on its own pass condition.
- A measured cause × month and age × month seasonal composition tensor, transferred to every country through the existing Köppen spatial-seasonality donor cascade; `makePersona()` now takes the simulated death's date.

**Re-sourced 2026-08-22.** 04-02 and 04-03 were rewritten after investigation: the national cause
cube moved to WHO's Global Health Estimates (keyless, CC BY 4.0, 183 countries, 19 disjoint
five-year bands, 175 leaf causes), because the WHO Mortality Database 04-02 originally targeted has
**zero rows** for Nigeria, Ethiopia, DR Congo and India, and the 16-chunk GBD cause export 04-03
originally described is infeasible at `max_rows_per_download: 100000`. GBD became the source only
for what WHO lacks entirely — subnational. That reversed the 04-04/04-05 dependency: 04-04 needs
04-05's region key to attach a regional pyramid to a cell, so 04-05 moved to wave 2 and 04-04 to
wave 4.

**04-03 rewritten around the API, 2026-08-25.** GBD's endpoints are ordinary HTTP and its dimension
metadata is served with no auth at all. Only `php/download.php` needs a bearer token, and the result
poll does not — so 04-03 became a script with one manual step rather than a UI session. The same
pass corrected the age set: GBD 2023 has no `1-4 years` group, so the disjoint selection is 22 ids,
not 21.

Deliberately **not** a plan: opportunistic subnational sourcing beyond Eurostat moved to backlog
999.1, because full subnational coverage is unreachable at any effort level and keeping it as a plan
implied committed work.

## Milestone Summary

**Decimal Phases:** None. Phase 6 was an integer insertion (2026-08-28) rather than a decimal, since
it followed Phase 5 rather than interrupting a phase.

**Key Decisions:**

- **Source selection is an investigation, not an assumption.** 04-02 and 04-03 were both rewritten mid-phase after the originally-named sources turned out to be unusable — the WHO Mortality Database has zero rows for four of the world's most populous countries, and GBD's cause export is infeasible under its own row cap. WHO GHE for national causes, GBD for subnational only.
- **Waves come from `files_modified` overlap, not from intuition.** Phase 4's ordering was derived pairwise, which is why `app/globe/persona.ts`'s three consumers ended up in three different waves.
- **A derived tier ships as a file aligned to `rate-grid.json`'s cell order, never a wider grid row.** The tier resolved is part of the output (`classId` + `tier` arrays), not an implementation detail.
- **A month-conditioned reweighting tensor is measured as `HarmonicCurve` objects** (order-4, mean-1) fit with the existing `pipeline.curve.country_curve_records`, so it composes with the timing curve's evaluate/blend/shift primitives instead of becoming a parallel representation.
- **The rule for a key space is "express every key space a consumer joins against"** — not "mirror `subnational-cdr.json`". This is what INT-01 cost the milestone, and it is now enforced by a build throw rather than by convention.
- **Accessibility cost accepted and recorded rather than hidden** (05-03): white ink on 3 of 5 proxy fills is below WCAG AA. Both alternatives were evaluated and rejected; the decision is documented in `palette.ts` and pinned by a test.
- **The cause residual ("other causes") is aseasonal by decision** as of 2026-08-28 — documented in code, in the shipped meta, and in the story's who chapter, rather than left as an unexplained 1.0 reweight.

**Issues Resolved:**

- **INT-01** — the UK's real GBD pyramids were unreachable. GBD publishes UK subnational units as NUTS-2, but `build-region-keys.ts` keyed every UK cell `adm1:GBR-nnnn` because it derived its NUTS-preferring country set from `subnational-cdr.json`, which has no `GBR` (the UK having stopped reporting to Eurostat). **0 of 41 keys joined**, and 226 UK cells silently used a derived tier at 1.09% of world expected deaths, with no output anywhere saying so. Fixed: 33 of 41 keys join, 217 of 226 cells resolve tier 0 carrying 99.96% of UK expected death weight, and the UK's cells span 2 archetypes 11.08pp apart — so the fix survives k-means quantisation rather than being real only at the join. Guarded by a build throw and by `scripts/build-age-sex-cells.test.ts`, both verified to fail with the fix reverted.
- **INT-02** — `data/eurostat-regional.json` was 1.77 MB, committed, and read by nothing, while its SUMMARY called it a build-time validation input. Its `ageSex` layer turned out to be exactly that; the only obstacle was a hardcoded ISO-3166-2 → `adm1_code` join. Wired: 271 NUTS-2 regions across 34 countries, 3.79pp mean error against 4.02pp for a flat national pyramid, beating the baseline in 15 of 34 countries. ITA 1.89 vs 3.05 and POL 1.51 vs 3.62 are independent evidence that the GBD regional join INT-01 restored is worth restoring.
- Two false SUMMARY claims corrected in place (04-04's and 04-06's), and `PersonaDemo.tsx`'s comment claiming parity with the globe's distribution — untrue since 04-04 and 04-07 — corrected to say what it actually reads.
- The verification backfill: 0 of 2 v2.0 phases had a VERIFICATION.md at first audit; 3 of 3 do now.

**Issues Deferred (accepted debt, 5 warnings):**

- **INT-03** — `data/region-keys.json` synced to `public/` with no runtime fetcher: a 392 KB dead browser payload. Cosmetic, no correctness impact; recorded rather than done so the removal is a deliberate act with its own commit.
- **INT-04** — 20-archetype quantisation flattens the regional signal for 19.43% of expected deaths. 164 of 226 grid countries resolve exactly one archetype across all their cells; Germany is 202 cells → 1. Of the 15 GBD tier-1 countries, ETH, JPN and ZAF still collapse. Raising `ARCHETYPE_COUNT` is a payload-versus-fidelity trade-off with no forcing function behind it.
- **INT-05** — no runtime alignment guard between rate-grid and age-sex-cells. `persona.ts:261` indexes `CELLS.classId[cellIndex]` with no length check, and neither build script is in `predev`/`prebuild`. Latent: it needs someone to rebuild `rate-grid.json` alone, which happens in a notebook and not in any automated path.
- **INT-06** — the coverage guard is not mirrored on the story side. `usePersonaTables.ts` declares `CauseTable` with no `coverage` field, so a future all-ages export would be rejected by the globe and still rendered by `PersonaDemo`/`AgeMix`.
- **INT-07** — `m49ForIso3` duplicated byte-identically at `lib/spatial-seasonality.ts:120` and `lib/fallback-proxy-assignment.ts:81`. A pure refactor with no failing test behind it.
