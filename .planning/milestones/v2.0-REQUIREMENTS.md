# Requirements Archive: v2.0 Persona Realism

**Archived:** 2026-08-28
**Milestone status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-08-28
**Outcome:** 8 requirements in scope — 4 satisfied, 4 partial, 0 unsatisfied

> This is a point-in-time archive of the v2 requirements as they stood at milestone close.
> For current requirements, see `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`.

**Defined:** 2026-06-28 (v2 block), STORY-01..03 added 2026-08-21
**Core Value:** Make the reality of global mortality feel immediate while staying statistically honest about timing, placement, and representative identity.

## Scope

The v2 requirement block holds 15 requirements across four groups. The **v2.0 milestone** scoped 8
of them: `PERS-01..03` and `STORY-01..03` fully, plus `REAL-01` and `REAL-03` partially, since the
Personas work needed the same artifacts (region keys on the grid, and the existing seasonal curves).

The remaining 7 — `REAL-02`, `REAL-04..07`, `ENG-01`, `ENG-02` — were never mapped to a v2.0 phase
and carry forward unchanged.

## Requirements in v2.0 scope

### Personas: Representative Identity Fidelity

**Feature:** Personas — deepens accuracy of _who_ is represented once the Globe has already placed a death in a country.

- [x] **PERS-01**: Cause-of-death distributions are country-specific **and age-banded** rather than falling back to the global IHME table. — **satisfied**
- [x] **PERS-02**: Age and sex distributions are region-specific rather than national. — **satisfied**
- [~] **PERS-03**: Persona composition varies with the season the globe is already simulating. — **partial** (56 of 90 cause labels resolve a curve)

### Story: Reading Experience

**Feature:** Shared — the scrollytelling story at `/roadmap`, which is the project's argument rather than its data.

- [x] **STORY-01**: Sections end on their argument. Expandable asides that restate caveats the surrounding prose has already made are removed rather than kept as a contents list. — **satisfied**
- [x] **STORY-02**: The return-to-globe gesture fires only on deliberate input. Scroll momentum arriving at the end of the page never triggers it, however hard the flick. — **satisfied**
- [~] **STORY-03**: Each seasonality proxy has a stable identity colour. Reordering the ranking does not repaint a strip, and neither does the story's sky changing underneath it. — **partial** (accepted WCAG AA failure)

### Globe: Realism Layers (partially touched)

**Feature:** Globe — deepens temporal and spatial accuracy of _where_ and _when_ deaths are generated.

- [~] **REAL-01**: Death placement or rates can use sub-national mortality data where available. — **partial by design**
- [~] **REAL-03**: Death timing can be modulated by seasonal mortality patterns. — **partial by design**

## Requirements carried forward (not in v2.0 scope)

- **REAL-02**: Death timing can be modulated by time-of-day patterns for relevant causes.
- **REAL-04**: Cause or rate weighting can account for climate or biome patterns.
- **REAL-05**: Real-time weather can modulate relevant causes during heat, cold, storms, or other conditions.
- **REAL-06**: Conflict or excess mortality overlays can elevate relevant causes in active conflict zones.
- **REAL-07**: Pandemic or epidemic event modes can adjust mortality rates for known periods or scenarios.
- **ENG-01**: A general automated test runner covers API fallbacks and critical browser smoke behavior.
- **ENG-02**: Frontend modules are split if roadmap/social/publishing features make `public/app.js` harder to change safely.

## Requirement outcomes

### Validated (4)

- **✓ PERS-01** — v2.0. The coverage flag is honoured in `pickCause` and guarded by 15 tests; `data/causes.json` ships 183 countries × 90 age-banded causes, including the four the WHO Mortality Database has no rows for. Evidence: `04-VERIFICATION.md` criteria 1 and 2.
- **✓ PERS-02** — v2.0. All 59,954 cells resolve a pyramid; tier mix 44.60 / 51.84 / 3.56. Validated against 4 national-statistics sources and, since 06-01, 34 Eurostat countries. The UK blocker is fixed and guarded. Evidence: `04-VERIFICATION.md` criterion 3.
- **✓ STORY-01** — v2.0. No slot in any of the three story files, no component on disk, `storyTranslations.test.ts` green. Evidence: `05-VERIFICATION.md` criterion 1.
- **✓ STORY-02** — v2.0. `SETTLE_MS` gate with `lastWheelAny` assigned before the gate, so an inertia stream can never satisfy it. Evidence: `05-VERIFICATION.md` criterion 2.

### Partial — recorded on measurement (2)

- **~ PERS-03** — month reweighting is live and southern-hemisphere re-phasing is real (AUS band 8: ×0.903 Jan vs ×1.246 Jul), and age reweighting covers every band. But 34 of 90 cause labels resolve no curve, including "other causes" at 34–52% of adult band weight. **Downgraded from "complete" during the audit**, because the evidence did not carry the claim. The gap is a mapping limit, not a measurement one: `chapter_of_cause_label()` derives from Eurostat's European cause list and cannot name tropical causes. The "other causes" residual is aseasonal by decision as of 2026-08-28.
- **~ STORY-03** — colours frozen and pinned by test. White ink is below WCAG AA on 3 of 5 fills (3.37 / 1.61 / 1.44) — an accepted decision with both alternatives evaluated and rejected, documented in `palette.ts`. **Also downgraded from "complete" during the audit.**

### Partial by design (2)

- **~ REAL-01** — the region key is baked and now correct for every tier-1 country, but sub-national data still does not feed the globe's *placement or rates*. The rate grid remains national plus density. Phase 4 is a Personas phase and touched `REAL-01` only where the Personas work needed the same artifact.
- **~ REAL-03** — Phase 4 added *composition*, not *timing*. Timing modulation already existed. Shares `PERS-03`'s unmapped-label limitation.

### Adjusted during the milestone

- **PERS-01's source changed mid-phase.** The requirement named "the global IHME table" as what to replace; the replacement was originally specified as the WHO Mortality Database, which turned out to have **zero rows** for Nigeria, Ethiopia, DR Congo and India. Re-sourced to WHO Global Health Estimates on 2026-08-22. The requirement text was not changed — only the plan that satisfies it.
- **PERS-02's tier 2 was rebuilt after the fact.** 04-04 shipped a CDR-gap proxy with a single degree of freedom; 04-09 replaced it with WorldPop 1km population × national rate for 72 countries, and recorded a partial verdict on its own pass condition rather than claiming a clean win.

### Dropped

None. `STORY-04..12` were never promoted to requirements — only the three the phase planned were,
so the requirement list never implied uncommitted work. The remaining story todos stay in
`.planning/todos/pending/`.
